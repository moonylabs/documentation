Moony Network

Overview

An open, permissionless network shaped by independent participation.

The Moony Network is the community of independent participants who build, integrate, and transact with Moony. No company manages it, no governance body steers it, and no participant holds privileged access. It grows organically, as people choose to take part on their own terms, advancing Moony as a digital public good.

No corporate management. No centralized governance. No privileged access.

#How an open network grows

Without a company directing it, Moony spreads the way open standards do, through independent decisions that compound. Three dynamics drive that growth.

Independent participation

Anyone can build, integrate, or transact without coordination or approval. A developer ships a tool, a merchant accepts payment, a user transacts, each acting on their own initiative.

Composability

Each contribution is a building block others can extend. Tools, integrations, and applications stack and interoperate, so independent efforts reinforce rather than compete.

Network effects

More merchants attract more users, more users draw more builders, better tools attract more merchants. Adoption compounds on itself with no central party required.
