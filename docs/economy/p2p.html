Moony Economy

P2P Payments

Peer-to-peer payments represent Moony's most direct utility.

Peer-to-peer payments are Moony's core utility. By combining a fixed supply with fast, final onchain settlement, Moony enables permissionless, low-cost, and transparent value transfer between individuals without intermediaries.

#Why It Matters

Legacy payment systems rely on layered intermediaries such as banks, processors, or card networks, each introducing cost, latency, and potential for control or exclusion. Transactions can be delayed, reversed, or denied entirely, with entire regions excluded from participation.

Direct Settlement

Transfers settle directly onchain between sender and recipient. No third party takes custody of funds at any point in the process, eliminating custodial counterparty risk.

Censorship Resistance

Valid transactions are processed by the network regardless of who initiates them. No central authority can selectively block, delay, or reverse transactions based on the identity of the parties, the nature of the transaction, or political considerations.

Global Accessibility

Anyone with internet access and a compatible wallet can participate. There are no geographic restrictions, account requirements, or identity verification barriers. The network operates 24/7, unaffected by weekends, holidays, or banking hours.

#Global Scalability

To make P2P viable at scale, both cost and throughput must support everyday usage. Moony operates on the Solana blockchain, making it suitable for daily transactions, not just high-value transfers.

Throughput & Latency

Solana processes thousands of transactions per second with sub-second finality, ensuring payments complete quickly even during peak usage. This makes Moony suitable for real-time payment scenarios where speed and reliability are critical.

Low Fees

Typical transfers cost fractions of a cent, with fees known at submission. This makes Moony economically viable as a medium of exchange for transactions of any size, removing cost as a barrier to everyday use.

Energy Efficiency

Solana's proof-of-stake consensus mechanism enables high throughput with minimal energy consumption per transaction. This efficiency ensures that Moony can scale to global adoption without the environmental costs associated with energy-intensive networks.

#Commerce

The same properties that make Moony effective for person-to-person transfers apply equally to commerce. Instant settlement, negligible fees, and permissionless access mean that any merchant, service, or platform can accept MNY without vendor agreements, processing intermediaries, or proprietary infrastructure. Adoption is not gated by institutional partnerships. It is driven by independent decisions to integrate an open standard.

Digital Commerce

Online merchants, applications, and digital services can accept MNY directly. Integration follows the same open token standards used across the Solana ecosystem, enabling checkout flows, subscription models, and in-app purchases without specialized payment infrastructure or third-party processors.

Point of Sale

MNY can be used for in-person transactions at physical merchants. Any device capable of generating or scanning a wallet address can serve as a point-of-sale terminal, enabling cash-like payments in the physical world with the same instant finality and zero fees as a digital transfer.

Open Integration

Because MNY follows standard token protocols on Solana, any developer can build payment acceptance into their application. There are no vendor agreements, no processing fees beyond network costs, and no gatekeepers controlling who can accept MNY as payment.

#Financial Inclusion

Billions of people remain underserved or entirely excluded by traditional financial systems. In many regions, the absence of reliable banking infrastructure leaves individuals unable to send money across borders or participate in even the most basic forms of commerce. High inflation and economic instability further erode purchasing power, making it difficult to preserve wealth or plan for the future.

Where banking does exist, high fees, slow settlement times, and rigid requirements place meaningful participation out of reach for many. Cross-border remittances can take days to arrive, cost double-digit percentages in fees, and are often impractical for small amounts, limiting economic mobility and reinforcing systemic barriers.

Moony's peer-to-peer architecture offers a different path, one that provides access to a credibly scarce, sound digital currency while bypassing the bottlenecks of legacy finance. By removing intermediaries and leveraging low-cost, high-speed blockchain settlement, Moony extends open participation to anyone with a mobile device at a scale and inclusivity level traditional rails cannot match.

Open Access

Anyone with a smartphone can receive Moony without bank accounts or gatekeepers. There are no credit checks, identity verification requirements, or minimum balance thresholds. This removes the traditional barriers that prevent billions of people from participating in the global economy.

Remittance

Affordable, near-instant settlement for cross-border payments. Unlike traditional remittance services that charge double-digit fees and take days to process, Moony enables families to send money across borders in seconds at a fraction of the cost.

Sound Money

Moony's fixed supply offers an alternative to local currencies subject to arbitrary expansion and devaluation. In regions where inflation or currency instability undermine everyday exchange, access to a transparent, inflation-resistant currency can provide a more reliable foundation for peer-to-peer payments.

#Using it today

Because the protocol is open, payments are not tied to any single app. The Flipcash mobile app is the simplest way to send and receive Moony right now, with familiar onramps and a polished experience, but it is one interface among many. Anyone can build a compatible wallet, point-of-sale tool, or application against the same Reserve, on the same terms.

Building your own? See the Architecture reference and the CLI for interacting with the Reserve directly.
