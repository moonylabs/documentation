# Moony

The official Moony website and documentation, available at [moonylabs.com](https://moonylabs.com).

## What is Moony

Moony (MNY) is sound digital money — an open currency governed by a decentralized, immutable program
on Solana. Its supply is fixed forever at 21,000,000 coins, and the mint authority was revoked at
deployment, so no issuer can inflate it, no operator can freeze or pause it, and no one holds
privileged access. It is permissionless, trustless, and censorship-resistant by design: anyone,
anywhere can hold it, send it, and spend it like cash, without asking permission.

At the core of the protocol is the Reserve — the trustless onchain mechanism through which all MNY
enters and exits circulation, exclusively in exchange for verifiable onchain capital. Every unit is
priced by the same deterministic curve, for everyone, and the capital deposited to acquire MNY stays
onchain as redemption liquidity that grows with the supply. That capital is held in USDF, a
fully-reserved US-dollar stablecoin, so circulating MNY is backed 100% by onchain capital and can be
redeemed through the Reserve at the curve price.

## Open by design

Moony was created by [Moony Labs, LLC](https://moonylabs.com) on open-source infrastructure — the
[Flipcash Reserve Contract](https://github.com/code-payments/flipcash-program), MIT-licensed by
[Code Inc.](https://github.com/code-payments) and
[audited by Sec3](https://github.com/code-payments/flipcash-program/blob/main/docs/audit_final.pdf).
Moony Labs deployed a single currency on that standard, named it Moony, authored this documentation,
and then relinquished control entirely: no keys, no admin authority, no upgrade or pause mechanisms,
no ability to intervene.

Moony Labs is the originator, not the operator. The protocol does not depend on any company for its
ongoing operation — it runs as neutral public infrastructure, governed only by immutable code.
